# Meeting Notes

A skill created by Hermes during the Skillmark demo, used to show an agent selling a skill it made itself.

## Use

Given a transcript:

1. Extract decisions as a bullet list.
2. Extract action items with an owner and a due date when stated.
3. Keep the summary under 200 words.
4. Do not invent owners or dates that are not in the transcript.

## Limits

Text transcripts only; no audio.
